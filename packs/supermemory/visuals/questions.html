Questions

How does the credit balance work?
Every plan comes with a monthly dollar balance. Each API call, whether storing memories, searching or indexing, draws from that balance at the rates above. Top-ups add to it whenever you like.
What is an SM token?
SM tokens measure billable content processed during ingestion. Supported updates to the same document bill the net-new token delta; search and other operations are metered separately.
What happens if I store the same content twice?
For supported updates using the same customId within the same organization, unchanged content is not billed again on the token meter. New content and separately metered operations can still incur charges. A new document identity or a full replacement can change how ingestion is billed.
Do unused credits roll over?
Subscription credits reset monthly. Credits you buy as a top-up never expire; they stay in your balance until you use them.
What happens when I run out?
Usage requires an available credit balance. You can purchase additional credits in the console. Paid plans also offer automatic top-ups with configurable purchase limits. These limits control automatic credit purchases; they are not a cap on your total invoice.
Which plans include advanced document extraction?
Scale and Enterprise include advanced PDF extraction: page-by-page OCR with descriptions of figures and diagrams alongside the text. It is selected automatically based on your organization's plan, with standard extraction as a fallback if needed. Free, Pro and Max continue to support standard PDF extraction, including OCR for scans. This plan distinction applies to PDFs, not every supported file type.
Can I self-host?
Self-hosted deployments are available on Scale and Enterprise. Enterprise also supports fully air-gapped deployments, where LLM inference may be the only outbound call, or none at all.
Do you offer startup or research credits?
Yes. Qualifying early-stage startups and academic research teams get the Scale plan free for three months, including its included usage, plus dedicated support.
