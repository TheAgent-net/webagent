### Coverage From Day One

Corgi gives companies in your network instant access to startup insurance from day one.

### Direct Underwriting

We underwrite directly to reduce broker friction and help companies save up to 30%, with policies that adapt from early launch through scale.

### Handled End to End

We handle everything from onboarding to claims so your customers, members, and clients can stay focused on building.

## How It Works

### Tell Us About Your Audience

Tell us who you support, what they need, and how you want to introduce Corgi. We shape the offer, partnership economics, and any co-marketing around your network.

### Get Your Partner Page

Within days you get a dedicated page with your branding, your offer, and one link your team can share anywhere.

### Your Network Gets Covered

Companies follow your link to get an instant quote with the discount applied automatically. We help them choose the right coverage and handle onboarding, renewals, and claims from there.

## FAQ

What does it cost to partner with Corgi?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Nothing. The partner program is free for organizations that work with startups and growing companies. You share Corgi with your network, and we handle everything else.

What do companies in your network get?keyboard\_arrow\_down

An exclusive discount through your partner page, instant quotes, and coverage that can satisfy investor, vendor, landlord, and enterprise contract requirements. Most teams go from application to covered the same day.

What does your organization get?keyboard\_arrow\_down

A co-branded page with an exclusive offer for your network, co-marketing opportunities, and a direct line to our team for insurance questions and support.

Does your team have to sell insurance?keyboard\_arrow\_down

No. There is nothing to sell and no quota. You share your link, and our team takes care of quoting, onboarding, and customer support.

How fast can the co-branded page go live?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Typically within a few days of our intro call. We set up your page and exclusive offer, and you can start sharing it immediately.

Who can apply?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Any organization that supports startups or growing businesses can apply, including VC funds, accelerators, banks, platforms, communities, service providers, and operator networks.

How is the discount applied?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Companies use your dedicated link or promo code when they get a quote, and the discount is applied automatically.

Is coverage available in every state?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Availability varies by state. See our [disclaimers page](https://www.corgi.insure/disclaimers) for full licensing details, or ask us on the intro call.
