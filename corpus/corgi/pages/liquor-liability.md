# Liquor Liability Insurance

Built for eligible veterans’ and fraternal lodges that sell, serve, or furnish alcohol through a licensed on-premises bar. Policy notes

The base form applies only to the operations and premises described in the Declarations. It does not include off-premises liquor events, catering, or mobile bartending unless a written endorsement changes that boundary. Delivery applies only when expressly shown.

Cannabis and THC beverages, professional services, employment practices, weapons, expected or intended injury, punitive damages, and other exposures are excluded or restricted. The required liquor license must remain in effect.

## How Liquor Liability Compares

Liquor Liability, Commercial General Liability, and off-premises event coverage address different operations and claim triggers.

![](https://www.corgi.insure/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fliquor-liability-bottles.png&w=3840&q=75&dpl=dpl_4zCe6eapdSiqsQSKyumtgvomB5tE)

### Liquor Liability

What triggers it: A covered occurrence involving bodily injury or property damage arising from selling, serving, or furnishing alcohol. Common scenario: A patron is allegedly overserved and later injures a third party. Key difference: It addresses the alcohol-service exposure that a standard general liability policy may exclude for a business in the alcohol trade.

![](https://www.corgi.insure/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fcgl.webp&w=3840&q=75&dpl=dpl_4zCe6eapdSiqsQSKyumtgvomB5tE)

### Commercial General Liability

What triggers it: Covered premises or operations claims involving bodily injury, property damage, or certain personal and advertising injury. Common scenario: A visitor slips at the lodge. Key difference: CGL addresses general premises and operations exposure; it should not be assumed to replace standalone liquor liability.

![](https://www.corgi.insure/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fhnoa.webp&w=3840&q=75&dpl=dpl_4zCe6eapdSiqsQSKyumtgvomB5tE)

### Off-premises liquor events

What triggers it: Alcohol service away from the declared premises, including catering or mobile bartending. Common scenario: A lodge serves alcohol at a separate event venue. Key difference: The base form does not include this exposure unless an issued written endorsement or separate placement provides it.

![](https://www.corgi.insure/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fgeneral-liability-gavel.webp&w=3840&q=75&dpl=dpl_4zCe6eapdSiqsQSKyumtgvomB5tE)

## Liquor Liability Glossary

Key terms from the program form, Declarations, and coverage summary.

OccurrenceAn accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same general conditions. Coverage applies to bodily injury or property damage that first occurs during the policy period.

Common CauseAll bodily injury and property damage sustained by one or more persons or organizations as the result of the selling, serving, or furnishing of alcoholic beverages to any one person. The form treats each Common Cause as a single occurrence for purposes of applying the limits, the retention, and the assault-and-battery sublimit.

Selling, Serving or FurnishingIncludes providing, giving, distributing, delivering, or pouring alcohol, and permitting alcohol brought onto the insured premises for consumption.

Declared PremisesThe location specifically described in the Declarations. The base program does not automatically follow operations to other locations or off-premises events.

SublimitA lower limit applying to a specific exposure, such as assault and battery. It is part of, not in addition to, the policy’s applicable limits.

Defense CostsLegal and related claim-defense expenses. Under this form they are included within, and reduce, the applicable limits and may exhaust them.

Policy AggregateThe most the insurer will pay for covered Loss and Defense Costs across the policy period, unless an issued endorsement changes how the aggregate applies.

Per-Occurrence LimitThe most the insurer will pay for covered Loss and Defense Costs arising from one occurrence, subject to all other policy terms.

Gross Alcohol ReceiptsAll amounts charged for selling, serving, or furnishing alcoholic beverages, including related service and corkage charges, used as a rating and premium-audit basis under the program materials. Separately stated taxes remitted to a governmental authority are excluded.

RetentionThe amount shown in the Declarations that the insured is responsible for under the policy before the insurer’s payment obligation applies.

## FAQ

What does this liquor liability form cover?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Subject to all policy terms, it may cover Loss and Defense Costs from third-party bodily injury or property damage claims arising from selling, serving, or furnishing alcohol, including certain intoxication, underage-service, and liquor-law allegations. The issued policy controls every coverage determination.

Who is this program designed for?keyboard\_arrow\_down

The current program materials are designed for eligible veterans’ and fraternal lodges operating licensed on-premises bars. Eligibility is determined through underwriting and may vary by state and risk characteristics.

What are the key limits and exclusions?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Limits and the retention are shown in the issued Declarations. Defense Costs are inside and reduce the limits. The base form is limited to the described premises and operations and excludes or restricts off-premises events, cannabis and THC beverages, knowing underage service for the insured who knew, weapons, professional services, employment practices, punitive damages, and other exposures. Review the complete issued policy.

Are endorsements available?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Potential endorsements include additional insured status, aggregate limit modification, and waiver of transfer of rights of recovery. These are not automatic and apply only when issued in writing following underwriting approval.
