# Insurance for Clubs & Lodges

Purpose-built liquor liability coverage for eligible veterans’ and fraternal lodges that serve alcohol on premises.

interactive dot field

### Made for Lodge Operations

Designed for eligible veterans’ and fraternal lodges with licensed on-premises bars and the day-to-day realities of member service.

### Focused on Alcohol-Service Claims

The policy may respond when selling, serving, or furnishing alcohol is alleged to have caused bodily injury or property damage, subject to issued terms, exclusions, limits, and retentions.

### Underwritten Around the Premises

Alcohol receipts, hours, entertainment, licensing, loss history, and the declared premises help determine eligibility and policy terms.

## Why Lodges Need Liquor LiabilityCoverage Shaped Around How the Bar Operates

### A Licensed On-Premises Bar

Alcohol service creates liability exposure that ordinary premises coverage may not address. The issued liquor liability policy controls.

### Members and Guests

Claims can begin with service to a member, guest, or allegedly intoxicated patron and continue after they leave the premises.

### Events and Entertainment

Hours, alcohol receipts, entertainment, security, and special events can affect both underwriting and the terms available to a lodge.

## Where Claims BeginAnd Policy Details Matter

Service to an Intoxicated Guest

### A Patron’s Ride Home

A patron is allegedly overserved, leaves the lodge, and later causes a collision involving bodily injury or property damage.

### Service to an Intoxicated Guest

A claimant alleges that alcohol service continued after a guest was visibly intoxicated.

### An Age-Verification Dispute

A guest presents identification, is served, and a later claim alleges underage service. The facts and any knowing-service exclusion matter.

### An Event Beyond the Lodge

The organization serves alcohol away from the declared premises. Off-premises events should not be assumed covered unless written coverage is issued.

## What a Claim Can Look LikeIllustrative Scenarios

### The Ride Home

After leaving the lodge, a patron is involved in a collision and the organization is accused of overserving them.

### The Private Reception

A guest presents identification, is served alcohol, and a later injury claim alleges that the guest was under legal drinking age.

### The Altercation

A dispute between patrons becomes an assault-and-battery claim, where the issued policy wording, limits, and any applicable sublimit determine available coverage.

## FAQ

Who is this program designed for?keyboard\_arrow\_down

The program is designed for eligible veterans’ and fraternal lodges with licensed on-premises bars. Eligibility is subject to underwriting approval and availability in the applicable jurisdiction.

What may liquor liability insurance cover?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Liquor liability insurance may respond to covered claims alleging bodily injury or property damage caused by selling, serving, or furnishing alcohol. The issued policy controls, including its declarations, limits, retentions, exclusions, and endorsements.

Is liquor liability included in general liability?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Do not assume that a commercial general liability policy covers claims arising from alcohol service. A separate liquor liability policy may be needed. Read more about the [liquor liability product](https://www.corgi.insure/liquor-liability).

Are off-premises events covered?keyboard\_arrow\_down

The base coverage should not be assumed to extend to off-premises liquor events, catering, or mobile bartending. Any broader coverage must be issued in writing and is subject to its own terms.

Can assault-and-battery claims be covered?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Assault-and-battery coverage may be available when it is included in the issued policy. Any applicable sublimit, exclusion, or endorsement controls how a claim is handled.

What information is reviewed during underwriting?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Underwriting may review the liquor license, declared premises, alcohol receipts, hours of service, entertainment, events, staff practices, training, and loss history. Additional information may be required for a specific risk.

Is coverage available in every state?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Availability varies by jurisdiction and risk. Coverage is subject to underwriting approval and availability. See our [disclaimers page](https://www.corgi.insure/disclaimers) for licensing information.
