When an enterprise customer asked for proof of [business insurance](https://www.corgi.insure/startup-insurance), Sky had to produce a policy quickly or risk stalling the deal.

The traditional path didn’t help. Sky spent time on Google, asking around, and researching providers, but getting a quote from most brokers takes forever, and finding a good one is harder than it should be.
