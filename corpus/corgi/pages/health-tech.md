## FAQ

What insurance does a health-tech startup need?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Health-tech startups need [Technology Errors & Omissions (E&O)](https://www.corgi.insure/blog/tech-eo-insurance-for-startups) for software liability, Cyber Liability with HIPAA-specific coverage for protected health information (PHI) breaches, [Directors & Officers (D&O)](https://www.corgi.insure/blog/do-insurance-secures-startup-future) insurance, and [General Liability](https://www.corgi.insure/general-liability). Corgi covers companies classified as Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) that may also need product liability or professional liability coverage.

Does my health-tech company need HIPAA compliance coverage?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Yes. If your platform stores, processes, or transmits protected health information (PHI), you are subject to HIPAA regulations. Corgi's cyber insurance with HIPAA-specific endorsements covers breach notification costs, regulatory defense, and penalties from HHS investigations. Most hospital and payor partners will require proof of this coverage before integration.

What is technology E&O for healthcare software?keyboard\_arrow\_down

[Technology E&O](https://www.corgi.insure/blog/tech-eo-insurance-for-startups) for healthcare software covers claims when your product causes operational disruption or harm in clinical settings. This includes allegations that your software produced incorrect clinical decision support, failed during critical care workflows, or caused a provider to make an error based on faulty data or system downtime. Corgi's policy is built for these high-stakes scenarios.

Do telehealth platforms need special insurance?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Yes. Telehealth platforms face unique risks including service interruptions during patient consultations, privacy concerns with video-based care, cross-state licensing compliance, and potential allegations of facilitating substandard care. Corgi covers both the technology platform risks and the regulatory complexities of delivering care across jurisdictions.

What cyber insurance do health-tech companies need for patient data?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Health-tech companies need cyber coverage that specifically addresses PHI breach response including HIPAA-mandated notifications, HHS regulatory defense costs, forensic investigation of healthcare data incidents, and business interruption from ransomware attacks targeting healthcare systems. Healthcare data breaches carry among the highest per-record costs of any industry. Corgi's [comprehensive package](https://www.corgi.insure/startup-insurance) is built for that severity.

Do I need product liability if I'm classified as Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)?keyboard\_arrow\_down

If the FDA classifies your software as SaMD, you usually need a Tech E&O policy with affirmative SaMD wording rather than relying on standard product liability. The classification tier (Class I, II, or III) drives how aggressive carriers will be on limits, exclusions, and clinical oversight requirements. See [Technology E&O](https://www.corgi.insure/tech-eo) for how Corgi structures coverage when your software is itself the medical device.

How is clinical trial insurance different from standard Tech E&O?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Clinical trial insurance is its own line. It covers bodily injury to trial subjects, sponsor-investigator liability, and protocol-related claims that Tech E&O explicitly excludes. If you're running or sponsoring trials (even decentralized digital ones), you need a dedicated clinical trial policy alongside your software E&O. Corgi can sit alongside a clinical trial program and cover the technology-platform exposures that the trial policy won't pick up.

What about FDA post-market surveillance and recall claims?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Post-market surveillance findings, MDR reporting failures, and software recalls can trigger both regulatory defense costs and third-party claims from affected providers or patients. Recall coverage and FDA investigation defense are usually endorsements you have to ask for. They're not automatic. See [Technology E&O](https://www.corgi.insure/tech-eo) and [Cyber Liability](https://www.corgi.insure/cyber-liability) for how Corgi structures the defense side of a post-market event.

What HIPAA-related coverage do I need beyond standard cyber?keyboard\_arrow\_down

Beyond a generic cyber policy, you want [Cyber Liability](https://www.corgi.insure/cyber-liability) with BAA-aligned wording, OCR investigation defense, regulatory fine coverage where insurable, and explicit PHI breach notification limits that match your covered-lives count. Most enterprise hospital and payer partners will ask to see this at integration, and the BAA you sign with them often references specific minimum limits. Corgi's health-tech cyber is built to satisfy those vendor requirements out of the box.
