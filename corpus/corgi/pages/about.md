# Building Thoughtful  Insurance Products

Corgi is an AI-native, full-stack insurance platform for technology companies. We started Corgi because business insurance is harder to navigate than it should be. By operating across underwriting, policy design, servicing, and claims administration, we can offer a better experience: clearer coverage, a faster path to a quote, and a more coordinated claims process.

We offer modular coverage that fits how modern businesses actually operate, so you can start with what you need today and expand as your risks change, without switching providers or stacking policies from different carriers.

Our goal is to rebuild the $1T+ insurance industry from the ground up. We're not just making a company; we're engineering financial infrastructure for the next hundred years.

## Built on a Secure Foundation

### Federally Regulated

Coverage is primarily underwritten by Technology Risk Retention Group (TRRG), a federally chartered risk retention group regulated under the Liability Risk Retention Act (15 U.S.C. §3901).

### A-Rated Admitted Access

For accounts where state-admitted paper is preferred or required, Corgi places coverage through partner carriers rated A- (Excellent) or better by AM Best.

### Licensed Nationwide

Corgi Insurance Services, Inc. holds producer licenses in all states where it operates (CA License #6012791).
