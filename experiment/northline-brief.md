## What can go wrong — and what pays
- Your product is down or gives bad advice and a customer sues → **Tech E&O**, typically up to $1M per claim / $2M aggregate.
- Customer data leaks and they sue you → **Cyber**, typically up to $1M per claim / $2M aggregate.
- A board or investor sues over a deal or fundraise → **D&O**, typically up to $1M per claim / $2M aggregate.
- Someone is hurt at your office or an event → **CGL**, typically up to $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate.

## Best fit
Pre-seed & Seed package: CGL, D&O, Tech E&O, Cyber.
Site cost-by-stage: about $2,000–$4,000 / year for ~$1M core limits on an eligible early startup.

## If you skip insurance
- First enterprise contract asks for a COI and walks if you have none.
- A claim without E&O or D&O can hit founders personally (D&O blog on the site).
