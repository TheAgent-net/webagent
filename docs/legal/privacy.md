## AI assistant

Our website has an AI assistant. [Company] operates it with [Provider], our service provider. The assistant answers questions about our products from the public pages of our website.

**What we store.** When you use the assistant, we store:

- the questions you type and the replies of the assistant,
- the page where you opened the assistant,
- a random session id that your browser keeps until you close the tab,
- the type of browser or agent (the user agent),
- a one-way hash of your IP address. We add a secret value before we hash it. We do not store your IP address.

We do not ask for your name or your email. Do not type personal data, passwords, or payment data into the assistant.

**Why we store it.** We use this data to answer your question, to send your request to our team when you ask for a person, to find and fix wrong answers, and to protect the service from abuse. Our legal basis is [legitimate interest / consent].

**Who receives it.** [Provider] hosts the assistant and processes the data for us under a data processing agreement. The assistant sends your question to an AI model provider, [model provider], to write the reply. [Model provider] does not use the data to train its models [confirm with the contract]. When you ask for a person, we send the conversation to our team by [email / Slack / webhook].

**How long we keep it.** We keep conversations for [90] days. Then we delete them. We keep counts without personal data for longer.

**Your rights.** You can ask us for a copy of your conversations, or ask us to delete them. Send the date and time of the conversation to [privacy contact email]. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

**Contact.** [Company name], [address], [privacy contact email].
